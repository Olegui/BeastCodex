# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- 704 hunter beasts from 19 families, tameable and untameable, grouped by appearance.
- Filters by family, zone and level range; untameable and map-location toggles; search in the game's language and in English; clear-filters button.
- 3D model preview, pinning, and world map pins on every spawn point.
- Hunter, Selva and Classic themes, switched live.
- 11 languages, with beast, zone and ability names from the game.
