# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- New pet: Ta'shkal, a green and orange cat in Mount Hyjal with 1.0 attack speed, summoned with the Corrupted Cat Figurine. Marked as a rumor until a data source confirms it.
- Target window: a small window of its own with a button that targets the beast once it is nearby. It stays open with the codex closed, follows the theme, keeps its position and reopens after a reload.
- News cards above the list (new pets, tips), which can be folded away.
- 3D model controls: turn left, turn right, play and pause.
- Search ignores apostrophes and quotes: "tashkal" finds Ta'shkal.
- Rounded drop-downs, hover that follows each button's shape, red glow on close buttons.

## 1.1.3

- Zone filter: each zone is listed once. Sub-areas of the same map no longer show as repeated entries, and picking a zone shows every beast in it.
- Dungeons, raids and the new Forever zones are named in the game's language instead of English.
- The Selva theme is now called Jungle.

## 1.1.2

- No changes to the addon. First version published to CurseForge straight from GitHub.

## 1.1.1

- New icon: a plain paw on a flat color, easier to tell apart at minimap size.
- The addon list groups it under "Assan", together with the author's other addons.

## 1.1.0

- Classic theme now uses the game's own interface: window frame, panels, red buttons, checkboxes, search box, drop-down menus and scroll bar.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- 704 hunter beasts from 19 families, tameable and untameable, grouped by appearance.
- Filters by family, zone and level range; untameable and map-location toggles; search in the game's language and in English; clear-filters button.
- 3D model preview, pinning, and world map pins on every spawn point.
- Hunter, Selva and Classic themes, switched live.
- 11 languages, with beast, zone and ability names from the game.
