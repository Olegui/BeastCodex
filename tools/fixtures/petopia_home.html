Welcome to our Forever site! Hopefully we can help you find the ideal companion to journey with you through World of Warcraft: Forever.
Note that the hunter pet information currently presented here (including pet abilities) was gathered during the limited Beta test and is therefore a work in progress. Plenty could change at launch, plus information on higher level and very rare pets was difficult to obtain during the test period. We'll continue to update as we get more info, but please let us know on our forums if you have any corrections or find any missing pets. Happy adventuring!
